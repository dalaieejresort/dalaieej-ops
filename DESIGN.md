Use this as a compact inspiration brief for an internal tool. The desired character is precise, editorial, quiet, and technical—not a conventional rounded-card SaaS dashboard.



## Core idea



Build the interface as a visible grid. Hairline rules create the structure; small typography, measured content widths, and deliberate empty space create hierarchy. Keep the product chrome monochrome and reserve color for status and one decisive action.



## Visual language



- White background, black text, gray metadata.

- Always use the light palette, regardless of the operating-system preference.

- `0.5px` gray dividers throughout.

- Square corners and no shadows, gradients, glass, or blur.

- Regular-weight sans-serif typography; use bold sparingly.

- Suggested scale: `24px` page titles and metrics, `12px` body and controls, `10px` metadata and actions, `8px` table/chart labels.

- Light-gray fills for hover and selection.

- Optional quiet dot grid behind canvases, previews, or visual assets.

- Cherry `#D2042D` for primary create/confirm actions and text selection, with white text. Primary actions darken to `#B80327` on hover. Inspector highlights and Bézier handles use the same accent token.



Core colors: black `#000`, white `#fff`, muted/hairline `#8c8c8c`, selected `#f0f0f0`, inset/hover `#f5f5f5`, disabled `#d9d9d9`.



## Layout recipe



- Fixed `40px` top bar.

- Compact `123px` desktop navigation rail with text labels and tiny numbered indices.

- `72px` page-title band beneath the header.

- Navigation rows are `24px` high on desktop and `36px` on mobile.

- Keep administrative content in a narrow left-aligned working column; do not stretch it across a wide monitor.

- Let dense collections fill the viewport as a flush grid with no gaps between cells.

- Let creation tools use the remaining viewport as a spacious canvas.

- Preserve empty space. It is part of the composition, not unfinished UI.



## Page patterns



**Workspace:** Large quiet canvas, one central prompt or task, compact floating controls.



**Library:** Full-width grid, touching cells, prominent indices, muted dates, preview wells, terse text actions.



**Operations:** Narrow measured column, section dividers, aligned labels and values, monochrome charts, flat tables.



## Components



- Buttons: square, `24–40px` high; cherry primary, black secondary, hairline outline.

- Inputs: transparent with a bottom border rather than a rounded box.

- Dropdowns: attached directly to their trigger; no gap or shadow.

- Tables: compact rows separated by hairlines; tiny muted headers.

- Modals: small flat panels over a dark scrim; no radius or shadow.

- Status: pair restrained green, orange, or red with a word or glyph.

- Hover/active states: change the fill; never lift, scale, or glow.



## Content tone



Use short, literal labels: Search, Newest, Usage, Create key, Add credits. Prefer sentence case, regular weight, and visible state changes such as Copied, Saved, or Generating… Avoid marketing language inside the tool.



## Guardrails



Do not add rounded cards, oversized bold headings, decorative icons, colorful panels, or excessive padding. Keep essential text at `10px` or larger, provide visible keyboard focus, and never rely on color alone.



The final result should feel like a carefully typeset working instrument: dense where comparison matters, spacious where focus matters, and visually calm everywhere else.
